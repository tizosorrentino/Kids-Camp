🧟 Apocalypse Tower City 🏙️

The Zombie Apocalypse Is Here!

Zombies keep pouring out of a stinky porta potty 🚽. Drive your tower down the long road. When zombies block your way, you can't run them over — you have to shoot them! Then blast that porta potty to pieces to win the level.

Watch the orange traffic cones! When a zombie walks past the cone closest to you, your car drives out to meet it.

You start with one wooden crate, one old car, and the gun in your hands. Shoot zombies to earn coins 🪙, then buy more crates, stronger crates, blasters, better cars, and new guns — pistols, MGs, shotguns, spamming guns, and snipers. Every level the zombies — and the porta potty — get tougher!

Zombies smash your crates first, then your car, then you. If they get you, you go back to level 1 — but you keep everything you bought!

Drive with A / D or the arrow keys. Aim with the mouse and click (or hold Space) to shoot. Press P to pause.
