ARCTIC ALIEN ASSAULT

Somewhere in the frozen north of Asia, the sky tore open.

You are the last soldier of the Northern Watch — a towering figure sealed inside bullet-proof dark green armor and a reinforced combat helmet. The aliens have landed in the ice fields. Hold the line, loot the crates, and survive — through all 20 checkpoints. Each level ends in a boss fight, and every boss is tougher than the last: the Level 1 boss has 10% health, and it climbs by 10% every level after that, all the way up to 200% on Level 20.
